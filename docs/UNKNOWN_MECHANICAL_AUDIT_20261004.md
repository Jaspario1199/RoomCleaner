# Mechanical unknown register — 4 October 2026

Read-only audit of the canted outlet experiment, fresh structural review, mount interface control, extended V3 build, compact V2 camera build, and `cad/clamp_extended_v3.py` (including inherited `clamp_v1.py` interfaces). This register distinguishes a CAD default from an achieved fit or physical rating. Component selection does not establish ordering, receipt, or assembly. No physical test results are supplied by this audit.

The bounded scope is four wall stations, cable/spool/encoder mechanics, removable docks, an extended two-jaw effector, electronics retention, and their manufacture/service envelopes. Motion/vision software implementation is outside scope; measured mechanical parameters needed by that software are included. Units below are mm, degrees, N, N·m, kg, °C and hours unless explicitly stated.

## Critical contradictions requiring decisions

1. **Outlet architecture is not frozen.** The structural review recommends articulation, the articulation experiment rejected its cable-routing geometry, and the canted experiment is disconnected from the mount and collides with its enclosure. Neither experiment supersedes the tested bench cartridge as a full-room assembly. Choose a validated restricted workspace first or complete a new structural outlet system; do not combine checks from different versions.
2. **A 55° cone has almost no installation margin.** The ideal complete corner orthant needs54.7356°, leaving0.2644° before mounting, cable-throat offset, body orientation, and room survey error. The actual feasible region can differ because suspension points are separated and the effector has volume. Nominal cone clearance is not room coverage.
3. **Rear and front cones are different.** The incoming winding corridor reaches63.626° in the canted prototype; a55° rear aperture failed24 samples. Enlarging the rear to65° solves those sampled paths but increases body volume and does not establish continuous tangency, rope abrasion, or load capacity.
4. **Axial bead tests do not establish oblique homing.** OD28 bead versusID26 collar gives only1 mm radial overlap when centered. At oblique approach its contact, centering, stroke and guide friction change. Larger diameter can increase side load or obstruct the room cone. A restricted homing pose is the recommended initial solution.
5. **Holding torque is not available running torque.** The unverified0.36N·m holding value cannot support40N at15mm radius (requires0.60N·m before losses). Encoders do not increase torque or detect line slip beyond the shaft. Set actual measured tension limits.
6. **Encoder assembly clearances are not centering accuracy.** Published fit floats can sum to0.45mm before printing/shaft errors, exceeding the≤0.25mm data-sheet centering condition. A measured locating/calibration process is necessary.
7. **Longer reach changes the suspended system.** V3 uses150mm extension and approximately268mm cable-plane-to-pad reach, not compact V2 reach/40° camera pose. It increases joint loads, swing clearance and center-of-mass sensitivity; the inherited electronics geometry is not evidence of tested retention or heat dissipation.
8. **Four cables cannot independently prescribe six pose coordinates.** Four tensile inputs do not generally guarantee commanded horizontal orientation and arbitrary translation simultaneously. Desired level equilibrium needs measured masses, anchor geometry, positive tensions and center-of-mass analysis. Tilt sensing measures orientation; it does not supply missing actuation.

## Methodical closure order

1. Freeze the applicable assembly versions and room/workspace/home requirements (M01–02), actual component revisions, and datums A motor axis/B motor face/C base back/D throat/E dock contact. Keep unknown measurements unset in the parameter skeleton; preserve illustrative CAD defaults in a separate provenance field.
2. Acquire measurements without claiming purchase status: motors/shaft, braid, ring, switch, springs/guides, encoder/magnet, servo/horn, camera, battery/modules, exact screws/connectors, substrate and printing coupons. These close geometric inputs rather than strength ratings.
3. Close the coherent outlet architecture and every service/installed mate, including failed canted bracket/cover/head seats and oblique or restricted home behavior. Then close drum/hub/encoder location and full rotating/tangent corridor.
4. Measure mass/COM, available running torque, spool radius and load envelope; calculate reactions for station, dock and extended effector. Set explicit allowable deformation from fit margins before structural testing.
5. Test real printed/hardware joints and thermal/duty envelope on restrained low-height fixtures, including reset, abrasion, creep and retention. Freeze only the tested material/profile/hardware revision. A software pass or a supplier component rating cannot close these physical gates.

Primary supplier links and limitations remain in the two source interface/structural documents; this audit performs no new supplier availability lookup and introduces no new purchased specifications.
