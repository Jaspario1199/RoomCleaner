## Recommended purchase candidate

BNTECHGO 18 AWG, 100 ft, red/black two-conductor parallel silicone wire, stranded tinned copper. Amazon ASIN B079CG33K6: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B079CG33K6 . Select the 18 AWG / 100 ft paired-cable variant.

Manufacturer page verifies copper construction and 100 ft paired length; displayed direct price was $37.98. Amazon exposed no featured offer or address-specific delivery estimate to search. Do not present the direct price as an Amazon quote or promise next-day delivery. This is the best verified flexible cable candidate identified in this search, not proof of the lowest delivered price.

Four nominal 14 ft station runs require 56 ft of paired cable before routing slack. The 18 AWG proposal applies to individual station runs, not automatic approval of a shared feed carrying all four stations. Final branch current, voltage drop, fuse coordination and terminal fit need verification. Silicone improves flexibility inside the housing and service loops; restrain and protect cable from abrasion. No building in-wall installation rating was established.

Alternatives reviewed: inexpensive Energeaster/VIABRICO/NAOEVO cables pasted by the user are CCA; WAGO documentation specifies copper, and approval of these CCA cables was not established. Fermerry 28 AWG is for small signal wiring, not the long station supply. TUOFENG 22 AWG was not selected for long power runs. MaxBrite B071HG6WGM 18 AWG/100 ft OFC PVC cable is a technically plausible alternative, but no comparable Amazon offer/delivery information was exposed.
