# Controls, electronics, vision and order unknowns — 4 October 2026

This is an unknown-parameter skeleton and independent source audit. It does not implement a new controller, purchase parts or qualify hardware. Values below distinguish current code defaults, nominal CAD and historical order flags from measurements. Fill measured values with method, uncertainty, date, hardware revision and approving test evidence; a blank is not zero.

## Architecture decision before parameter filling

Three separate profiles exist. Keep their wiring and commissioning instructions explicit rather than combining them:

| Profile | What actually exists | Recommended current use |
|---|---|---|
| Central Uno + CNC shield + four remote encoder nodes | One scalar acceleration clock distributes four-axis pulses; encoder relay and watchdogs; host planner; detached homing/setup-pose procedure | Existing coordinated development baseline. Its point-effector geometry still requires replacement/qualification for the real four-anchor claw. |
| Experimental local XIAO-C3 + DRV8825 per station | Detached single-axis USB commands, encoder/driver/heartbeat guards, filtered homing and endpoint settling | One unloaded/restrained station. Four independent sketches do not constitute coordinated motion. Electronics packaging remains experimental. |
| Extended claw, XIAO-S3 Sense + IMU + battery + lower servo | Upper camera pod and electronics, extended printed frame, conditional camera/servo firmware, refreshed diagnostic JPEGs | Mechanical fit and local camera/servo/tilt bench tests. Moving-camera room missions are not integrated. |

Recommendation: qualify one local station while preserving the central baseline. Choose the intended four-axis execution architecture only after the local hardware, timing and power results are available. Local motor power packaging and coordinated control are separate decisions.

## Contradictions requiring reconciliation

1. `roomcleaner/config.py` still advertises only two room edits while mass, wall outlet coordinates, finite claw anchors, physical limits and pose also require calibration. Its old0.45kg mass/ideal ceiling corners are not the extended room robot.
2. `hardware/hw_config.py` requests20mm/s travel, but `driver.py` caps at500steps/s; at its constant20mm core the effective maximum cable rate is≈9.82mm/s. Retimed executor waypoints do not preserve the sampled trajectory timing.
3. `ENCODER_AND_TILT_COMMISSIONING.md` main steps still flash old ESP32 servoGPIO13/IMU21,22. Selected S3 Sense conditional firmware uses servoGPIO1 and IMU5,6. These can remain supported alternatives, but need explicit separate profile tables before wiring.
4. `BOM_ORDER.md` still calls itself final and contains old five-finger/overhead-camera/ceiling-anchor parts plus claims that a switched power strip performs the E-stop job,15N peak tension, ample12V5A power and≥50N·cm motor requirement. New docs partially supersede it; historical prose must not be treated as qualification or purchase instruction.
5. `ORDER_STATUS_V2.csv` retains superseded guide/spring candidates and M3×6 cap screws with1mm spacers. `FRESH_PARTS_DELTA_20261002.md` instead selects SHS3-12/Z-2CS, M3×5 cap screws without spacers and recessed M3×6 grub screws. CSV entries must be reconciled before generating an order.
6. The camera/claw selection is approved intent, not a receipt. Battery/charger, regulators, sensors, magnets and revised fastener availability remain distinct inventory questions. An M3 assortment described as6–30mm does not establish possession of M3×5 cup screws.
7. Local electronics profile advertises nominal full-body clearances while its fuse14AWG lead has no qualified roof bend/service route. Its document correctly restricts final printing/powered assembly; a one-plug concept does not close that packaging gap.
8. Encoder availability, tilt gating and camera retry code do not close actual payload-pose, cable-slip, room-leveling or moving-camera verification gaps. Each sensor constrains different unknowns.

## Methodical fill order

**A. Freeze profiles and inventory.** Reconcile O01 and conflicting wiring/fastener/order records; retain explicit measured/nominal status.

**B. Qualify one detached motor station.** Fill C03–C07 and E01/E02; compile and hardware-test the actual local adapter if that profile is selected. Measure real springs/bead travel, current, thermal response, slip and winding before assigning a working force/speed envelope.

**C. Qualify the complete supported claw.** Fill C01/C02/C12 and E03; measure actual CG, extension reach, IMU alignment, servo load, battery protection and camera-visible payload envelope.

**D. Close model, limits and coordination.** Solve C05/C08–C11 from measurements. Demonstrate supported workspace and machine-wide faults before suspended payload testing. No step skips the other axes' health/readiness requirements.

**E. Close camera evidence.** Calibrate V01 and measure V02; validate V03/V04 with negative cases before enabling scan/pick/deliver missions. Keep delivered count gated by positive camera evidence.

**F. Freeze an order and print configuration only after its dependencies close.** Structural proof/creep and exact hardware fit are separate mechanical gates handled by the mount audit. This ledger contains no new hardware qualification, purchase or performance guarantee.
