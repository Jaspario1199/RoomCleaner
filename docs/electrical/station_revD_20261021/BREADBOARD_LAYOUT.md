# Station Rev D on a breadboard (full-size 830-point)

Drawing: [breadboard_layout.png](breadboard_layout.png) / [.pdf](breadboard_layout.pdf). It is generated by
`breadboard_layout.py`, which also net-checks the layout against the perfboard and KiCad net list: 16 nets,
55 pins and 6 no-connects; it prints PASS only on an exact match. Deliberately wrong layouts (a pin on the wrong
rail, a pull-up in the 12 V row, 12 V on a rail) print FAIL.

Board convention:
- Rows 1–63 run left to right. Columns a–e are the top half and f–j the bottom half.
- **Both + rails are 3V3 and both − rails are GND.** Bridge + to + and − to − at the far end. Check with a meter
  that each rail is continuous: some boards split the rails in the middle.
- **12 V is on no rail.** It lives only in row 20, f–j (the A4988 VMOT row), and on the red jumper j20 → j36.

## Jumpers

- **Signals:** c6 → c26 (STEP), c7 → c27 (DIR), c8 → c20 (EN).
- **Microstep pins:** b21, b22, b23 → T− (MS1–3 to GND, full step).
- **Reset and sleep:** a24 → T+ (RST), a25 → T+ (SLP).
- **XIAO power:** j6 → B− (XIAO GND), j7 → B+ (XIAO 3V3).
- **A4988:** j26 → B+ (VDD), j21 → B− (motor GND), j27 → B− (logic GND).
- **12 V to the buck:** **j20 → j36, red. This is the only 12 V jumper.**
- **Buck ground:** j34 → B− (buck OUT−), j35 → B− (buck IN−).
- **Rail bridges:** T+ ↔ B+, T− ↔ B−.
- **JP1 = a removable jumper j29 → i5.** Plug it in only when running from 12 V with USB unplugged; leave it
  out whenever USB is connected.

## Bring-up

1. **Buck first.** Fit only the buck, D1 and the 12 V leads. Set the buck to 5.00 V (meter at h33) before the XIAO
   or JP1 go in.
2. **Logic check.** Add the XIAO (USB, JP1 out) and the A4988 with the motor unplugged. Check about 3.3 V on both
   + rails and EN high.
3. **Current limit.** Set Vref to the pot's maximum, about 0.85 V, which gives about 1.06 A.
4. **Motor.** Power off, plug the motor into j22–j25, then power on.

Breadboard notes:
- Each motor coil carries about 1 A through breadboard contacts. Keep the leads short and fully seated.
- A motor wire that pops out under power can destroy the A4988, so tape the motor wires down.
- Never unplug the motor with power on.
