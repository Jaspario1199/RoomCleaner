# Station Rev D on a breadboard with an ELEGOO ESP32 DevKit (2026-10-22)

For the station that replaces a fried XIAO. The circuit is the same Rev D circuit; only the controller board changes.
The XIAO version, [BREADBOARD_LAYOUT.md](BREADBOARD_LAYOUT.md), is unchanged.

Drawing: [breadboard_layout_esp32.png](breadboard_layout_esp32.png) / [.pdf](breadboard_layout_esp32.pdf). It is generated by
`breadboard_layout_esp32.py`, which runs the same net check as the XIAO layout (16 nets, 55 pins, PASS on an exact match)
plus a check that every ESP32 jumper lands on the right net.

**Why the ESP32 sits off the breadboard:** the 30-pin DevKit is about 25-28 mm wide. On one breadboard it covers every free
hole on at least one side, so you could not reach those pins. It stays beside the board and **nine female-to-male jumpers**
go from its header pins into the holes the XIAO's pins used.

## The nine jumpers (female end on the ESP32 pin, male end in the board)

| ESP32 pin (silkscreen) | GPIO | Breadboard hole | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| D25 | 25 | d6 | STEP (to A4988 STEP via c6 → c26) |
| D26 | 26 | d7 | DIR (via c7 → c27) |
| D27 | 27 | d8 | EN (via c8 → c20; R1 10k pull-up keeps the driver OFF at boot) |
| D32 | 32 | h8 | HOME (switch NC at i8, R4 10k pull-up at j8) |
| D21 | 21 | d9 | SDA (the encoder's SDA is at a9) |
| D22 | 22 | d10 | SCL (the encoder's SCL is at a10) |
| GND (either GND pin) | – | h6 | GND (j6 → B−) |
| VIN | – | h5 | 5 V from the buck through D1 and JP1 (j29 → i5) |
| 3V3 | – | T+ rail, row 19 | 3.3 V for the A4988 logic and the AS5600 (both + rails are 3V3) |

Everything else matches the XIAO breadboard layout: the A4988, buck, D1, C1, R1-R4, the switch, the motor, the 12 V leads, the
encoder and all other jumpers. The only exception: the XIAO's `j7 → B+` jumper is not needed, because the ESP32's 3V3 goes
straight to the rail.

**Pins to leave alone on the ELEGOO board:**
- GPIO0, 2, 5, 12, 15 (boot strapping);
- VP/36, VN/39, 34, 35 (input only, no pull-ups);
- TX0/RX0 (USB serial).

None of the six signal pins above is one of these. All six float at reset, so the EN pull-up holds the driver off until the
firmware arms it.

**AS5600 (UMLIFE board):** VCC → T+, GND → T−, SDA → a9, SCL → a10. Also tie its **DIR pad to T− (GND)**, as on Rev D sheet 3;
the AS5600's DIR input must not float. Leave OUT and GPO unconnected.

## Firmware

`firmware/winch_station/winch_station.ino` picks its pin map from the board selected in the Arduino IDE (one sketch for both):
- XIAO: board "XIAO_ESP32C3".
- **ELEGOO: board "ESP32 Dev Module"**, arduino-esp32 core 3.x, CP2102 USB driver. If the upload stalls at "Connecting...", hold
  BOOT until it starts.
- In `secrets.h` set this unit's `STATION_ID` (the station it hangs as, e.g. `C`), Wi-Fi and static IP. Put that IP:port under the
  same station in `config/robot.json`. Nothing on the host changes: it does not care which board a station uses.

**Not compiled here** (no Arduino toolchain in this workspace), same as the XIAO build. First compile and flash on your laptop,
then check on the bench before the motor goes on:
1. **Power:** with USB only (JP1 out), 3V3 on both + rails, and EN (d8 / c20) high = driver disabled.
2. **Boot:** reset the board and watch the shaft; with the motor plugged in and 12 V on, a reset must not move it.
3. **Commissioning:** steps 2-4 with `tools/commission_station.py` on an unloaded spool, as for the XIAO stations.

## Bring-up (same order as the XIAO)

1. **Buck:** fit only the buck, D1 and the 12 V leads. Set the buck to 5.00 V (meter at h33) before the VIN jumper or JP1 go in.
2. **Logic:** ESP32 on USB (JP1 out) and the A4988 with the motor unplugged. Check 3.3 V on both + rails and EN high.
3. **Current limit:** set Vref as for the other stations.
4. **Motor:** power off, plug the motor into j22-j25, power on.
5. **Running from 12 V:** unplug USB, then plug JP1 in. Never have JP1 in with USB connected.
